# POISON Client: all features

All features are visual, HUD, quality-of-life or self-only. There are no cheat modules.

## Launcher (Windows portable .exe)
- Microsoft account login (Microsoft → Xbox → Minecraft), with tokens saved encrypted.
- One-click install: Java runtime, game files, assets and Fabric / Legacy Fabric are downloaded and verified automatically.
- Versions: 1.21.4 (Fabric) and 1.8.9 (Legacy Fabric). Vanilla is also selectable.
- Separate instance folder per version, and the POISON mod is auto-installed into it.
- Quick Play buttons (Bedwars, Skywars, Duels, UHC, Practice, Survival) that join a server straight away.
- Pages: Home, Client preview, Modules, Locker, Music, Market, Settings.
- Live client preview with render presets (Cinematic, Neon Overdrive, Competitive, Vapor), knife flip/inspect, F5 and autopilot.
- Motion Blur Lab and Post FX: bloom, chromatic aberration, cinematic bars, filmic tonemap, color grading.
- Locker: butterfly knife skins and cosmetics, plus on-chain style badges.
- Built-in music player (POISON FM: shuffle, repeat, prev/next).
- Crypto vibe:
  - Simulated price ticker (not real prices).
  - $PSN play-money with a daily airdrop and a proof-of-click miner.
  - Market page.
- Settings: accent colors, background, FX quality, memory, Java path, JVM args, resolution, client menu key.
- Patch notes and stats (modules on, launches, badges).
- Frameless glowing UI with animated particle background.

## In-game client: 1.21.4 (42 modules)
The menu opens with Right Shift. Middle-click any module in the menu to bind it to a key. Settings save automatically.

**Render**
- Theme: mint (default), poison, toxic, vapor, gold, blood, ice, rainbow. HUD layout mint/classic. Glow intensity setting.
- Screen Glow: neon edges or full ambience, pulses with the music.
- Item Glow (G): held items glow like neon crystal.
- Block Outline: glowing outline plus fill.
- Motion Blur (M): frame-accumulation render-video look, with strength and FPS-independent settings.
- Self Chams (H): fill, outline or both on your own player only, with a color picker.
- Item Chams: neon shell on the knife, with a rainbow option.
- Time Changer, No Hurt Cam, Low Fire.

**Effects**
- Jump Circles, China Hat, Trails (N), Aura Ring.
- Hit Effects (J): crystal, sparks, coins, hearts or hex, with an adjustable particle count.
- Kill Effects: vortex, liquidation, lightning, confetti, rekt.

**Animations**
- Butterfly Knife: swords become an animated balisong.
  - Flip with R and inspect with I.
  - Skins.
  - Idle styles: still, chatter, twirl, aerial.
  - Auto-flip, flip speed, and flip open when you switch to it.
- Item Position: move and scale the held item.

**Camera**
- Zoom: hold Z, scroll to adjust, smooth.

**HUD**
- Info Bar: ping, server, TPS, name, clock.
- Music Card: spinning vinyl and progress bar.
- Keybinds panel.
- Custom Hotbar: rounded slots, slim health and hunger bars, offhand and armor boxes, inventory preview, hide XP.
- Watermark (glow, box, minimal) and Custom Crosshair (cross, cross-dot, circle, diamond).
- Array List: wave, accent or rainbow, with background and glow options.
- FPS, CPS, Ping, Coordinates, Clock, Keystrokes, Armor Status, Now Playing, Crypto Ticker.
- Pill notifications when you toggle a module.

**Music**
- Music Player: built-in tracks plus your own .ogg/.wav files.
  - Volume, shuffle, autoplay.
  - Keys: [ previous, ] next, \ pause.
- Lyrics (K): synced lyrics pill, plus giant floating 3D lyrics in the world.
- Beat-Reactive Glow: the HUD pulses with the bass.

**Crypto / Utility**
- Gas Meter (ping in gwei).
- HODL Mode: press Q twice to drop, so you never drop your sword by accident.
- Toggle Sprint and Notifications.
